# Problem, audience and success criteria

**Problem.** Can a transparent predictive-maintenance scenario model connect data quality, failure risk, operating cost and the option to act or defer?

**Audience.** Industrial operations or investment decision-maker evaluating a scenario prototype.

**Success criterion.** The model compares a naive baseline with a risk-triggered policy, reports economic assumptions, tests degraded data and separates estimated value from observed fact.

Out of scope: production deployment, client outcomes, regulatory approval and any claim not present in the evidence manifest.
