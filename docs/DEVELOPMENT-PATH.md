## Layers to develop

### Business

Choose one asset class, failure mode and decision owner. Establish the economic job, alternative solutions, data-access constraint, deployment boundary and buyer value before returning to a broad industrial platform thesis.

### Technology

Develop telemetry ingestion, data-quality controls, feature computation, risk forecasting, uncertainty calibration, policy optimization, scenario/real-option engine, explanation and monitoring as separately testable modules.

### Operations

Model inspection, work-order planning, spares, maintenance windows, override authority, incident response, model drift and post-maintenance learning. Optimize the human decision loop, not only model accuracy.

### Finance Risk

Connect downtime, maintenance cost, failure severity and capital timing to NPV and options to defer, stage, expand or abandon. Run sensitivity to data quality and forecast calibration.

### Governance

Define sensor and maintenance-record provenance, safety-critical overrides, access, auditability, change control and when a model must abstain.

## Execution sequence

1. Select one public industrial or energy dataset and document its operational proxy limits.
2. Compare naive, statistical and one machine-learning forecast under temporal validation.
3. Calibrate failure risk and test policy value across asymmetric cost scenarios.
4. Add work-order and human-override states plus drift/data-quality alerts.
5. Write an investment and operations memo that separates modeled option value from realized impact.
