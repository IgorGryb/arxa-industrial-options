# Hypotheses, prediction and falsification

## Primary hypothesis

The model compares a naive baseline with a risk-triggered policy, reports economic assumptions, tests degraded data and separates estimated value from observed fact.

## Prediction recorded before test

The risk-triggered policy will have lower modeled expected cost than never performing preventive maintenance, while degraded data will not improve forecast error.

## Falsification criterion

The prediction is falsified if modeled policy cost is not lower than baseline or if degraded-data error is lower than full-data error.

## Alternative explanation

A passing result may follow from the deliberately narrow fixture or chosen assumptions rather than general capability. External validity therefore remains unconfirmed.
